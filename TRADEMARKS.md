# Scrubjay trademark policy (draft)

> **Draft, pending legal review.** The name was chosen in D-035, pending the trademark search, and no trademark has been
> filed yet. This draft states the intended policy (D-020).

The Scrubjay **code** is free software under the AGPL-3.0-or-later. The **name "Scrubjay" and its
logos** are not covered by that licence. They identify the official project.

**You may**, without asking:
- say that your software works with, imports from or exports to Scrubjay;
- refer to Scrubjay by name in articles, reviews, tutorials and teaching material;
- use the name for unmodified official builds you redistribute.

**You may not**, without written permission:
- distribute a modified version (a fork) under the Scrubjay name or logo: please choose a
  different name and say it's "based on Scrubjay";
- use the name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement, or in a product or domain name;
- offer a hosted service under the Scrubjay name.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
